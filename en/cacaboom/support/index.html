Help and support

A question, a problem, an idea? You are in the right place.

How to play

  1. Slide your finger to aim, then let go to drop the poop into the toilet.
  2. Two matching poops that touch merge into a bigger one.
  3. Two dino poops make the Big Boom, which charges up and explodes: CACABOOM!
  4. Keep your poops below the dotted line. In an emergency, flush: it sweeps away the small poops. Score points to refill it.

Frequently asked questions

I can’t hear any sound

First, check that your iPhone is not in silent mode: that is the switch on the side of the iPhone (or the Action button on recent models). Then turn the volume up.

Also check that the Sounds switch is on: you will find it in the Pause menu and on the end-of-game screen.

How do I turn off vibration?

Tap the Pause button during a game, or wait for the end screen: the Vibration switch turns it off or back on. Your choice is kept for the next games.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

The leaderboard and achievements use Game Center. Sign in under Settings > Game Center on your iPhone, then relaunch Cacaboom. Tap the trophy on the home or end screen to see the leaderboard.

When do I lose?

When a poop stays resting above the dotted line for the whole 3, 2, 1 countdown. If it drops back below the line in time, you are saved.

Tip: flush to make some room.

What is the CACABOOM?

When two dino poops merge, they make the Big Boom. It charges up for a moment, then explodes, blasting the poops around it: lots of points and plenty of room.

Is the game really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account to create. It even works offline.

I lost my best score

Your best score is saved only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, it is erased, unless you restore an iPhone backup. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score stays visible on the leaderboard.
