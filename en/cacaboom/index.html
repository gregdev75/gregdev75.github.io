How to play

  1. 1

    Aim and drop

    Slide your finger to aim, then let go: the poop drops into the toilet.

  2. 2

    Merge

    Two matching poops that touch merge into a bigger one. Chain merges to score combos.

  3. 3

    CACABOOM!

    Two dino poops make the Big Boom. It charges up… then explodes, blasting the poops around it.

  4. 4

    Stay below the line

    A poop resting above the dotted line starts a 3, 2, 1 countdown. Quick, flush!
